Directed by Carmen Vásquez Uriol.
Produced by Karoline Pelikan, Adriana García, Leticia Castañeda – Vuyana, Pelikan Pictures.
Sound Post-Production by Carmen Vásquez Uriol, Cocompi – Adoro el Sonidito Records.

A contemplative look at the town of Contumazá, in the northern highlands of Peru. Starting with a murder that took place a century ago —involving the director's great-grandfather— the film reflects on the passage of time through her mother's daily routine, conversations about a nearly forgotten event, and the faces of the town's inhabitants. The town's landscapes and traditions gradually reveal the beauty, solitude, and poetry of its people.

Official Selection in Festival Internacional de Cine Lima Alterna
